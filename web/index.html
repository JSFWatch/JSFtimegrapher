Time your watch on the bench.

Rate, amplitude and beat error from a piezo pickup or microphone. Everything runs in this browser tab, nothing is uploaded.

Your sound card's clock is checked before the first measurement. It takes about 10 minutes with a quartz watch, or 1 minute with a GPS timing signal.

Calibrate your sound card

Every 1 ppm of clock error is 0.09 s/d of rate error, so measuring stays locked until this is done for this input.
